## MAPE 
It stand for Mean absolute percentage error, we calculate the erro ratio of each line item then take the mean of all the errors, which is kind of wrong, 
- so it will treat all your line items equal , 50 % in low volumne items and 50% error in high volumn items is same. but it should not.
## WMAPE
It is the gold standard metric for retail supply chain and Central distribution centre (CDV) inventory planning. 
$$\text{WAPE} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \vert{}A_i - F_i\vert{}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} A_i}$$
it solves the issue of zero divsion of actual demand, we agg all the absolute erros and divide it by total demand 
## sMAPE
it stands for symmetric Mean absolute percentage Error (sMAPE). so for over forecasting we can have unbounded error like even 200% or more. but in case of overforecasting we limited to 0 value. so to fixed this asymmetric we need to divide by the average of actuals and forecast value   
$$\text{sMAPE} = \frac{100\%}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{\vert{}A_i - F_i\vert{}}{\frac{\vert{}A_i\vert{} + \vert{}F_i\vert{}}{2}}$$
