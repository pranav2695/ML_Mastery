## SVM
In 2-D plane we create the line which seperate the two classes, we want to maximize the margin lines between the classes. 
## Types of Margin 
1. Hard Margin : Assume we have prefectly separable data with zero erors A single outlier can ruin the model. it will have low bias and high variance
2. Soft Margin : Real data is noisy. we introduce slack variables to allow few points to sit inside the margin boundaries or opposite of decision boundary. they will have high bias but low variance.
