## Bagging 
It is made up of two words Boostraping and aggregation.  
we will do the sampling and train on the same base models, sampling we can do with replacement and without replacement. 
for the prediction we will give new point to all the base models and take the majority count.  
we use the base models which are Low bias high variance in bagging technique. for eg fully grown DT.  
variance is less in the bagging technique becasue new data points dont go in only one base models, impact will spread.  
- Bagging can be used for any algos not just DT.
## Types of Bagging 
- Pasting : here we do row sampling with out replacement 
- Random subsapces : we do column sampling (with or without replacement)
- Random patches : we will do the row and column sampling both.  
## OOB score (out of bag sample )
they are 37 percent of row which is not selected in training , these we can run to test the accuracy.  
for the regressor bagging it is same as classification bagging technique
One of the advantages of the out of bag score is that it allows us to estimate of the prediction error without needing a separate validation set. This can be particullarly useful when the dataset is small and partitioning it into training and validation sets might leave too few samples for effective learning.
